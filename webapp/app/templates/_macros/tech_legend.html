📊
RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Momentum meter, 0–100 scale. Above 70 = overbought (pullback risk). Below 30 = oversold (bounce setup). 40–60 = neutral. Uses Wilder's 14-day smoothing.
🎯
BB (Bollinger Bands)
Volatility bands around a 20-day average. Position 0% = at lower band (support). 50% = middle. 100% = at upper band (resistance). Above 100% = extended, pullback watch. Below 0% = extreme oversold, bounce watch.
⚡
MACD (Moving Average Convergence-Divergence)
Trend confirmation. Positive + rising (↗ +) = bullish momentum. Negative + falling (↘ −) = bearish momentum. Turning up from below zero = potential reversal.
📈
50 / 200-SMA
Trend direction. Above both = uptrend. Below both = downtrend. Golden cross (50 above 200) = bullish long-term. Death cross (50 below 200) = bearish long-term. Slope shown as %/month.
🟢
Support / Resistance clusters
Prices where the stock has reversed multiple times. Green lines = support (buyers step in). Red lines = resistance (sellers step in). "Touches" counts how many times the level was tested — more touches = stronger.
📉
ATR (Average True Range)
Typical daily price move as % of spot. Higher = more volatile. Used for sizing stops (e.g., stop = 1.5 × ATR below entry).
📏
52-week range position
Where spot sits between yearly low (0%) and high (100%). 90%+ = near highs. 10%− = near lows. Useful mean-reversion context.
💥
ATH drawdown
How far off the all-time high (negative %). −30% or worse suggests a "broken" chart — the fundamental thesis needs a fresh check, not just chart-reading.
🎬
Short-term (1-4 weeks) verdict
Fast read: stretched = pullback risk (RSI > 70 or BB > 90) · oversold — bounce = RSI < 30 or BB < 10 · bullish/bearish momentum = MACD-driven · stabilizing = downtrend fading · neutral = consolidating.
🧭
Long-term (3-12 months) verdict
Slower read: secular uptrend = above 200-SMA + Golden cross + rising 200 · uptrend intact = above 200-SMA + Golden cross · broken chart = drawdown > 30% and below 200-SMA · confirmed downtrend = Death cross + falling 200 · weakening, recovery attempt, sideways = in-between states.
How to combine signals: A single signal is noise. Three converging is a setup.
  • Classic bounce: RSI oversold + BB below lower + spot at support
  • Pullback risk: RSI overbought + BB above upper + spot at resistance
  • Trend restart: MACD turning up from below zero + spot above 200-SMA

Not investment advice — technicals are one input. Combine with fundamentals, position sizing, and risk management before acting.
